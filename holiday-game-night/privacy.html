Holiday Game Night Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 August 2026

Holiday Game Night is designed to be played locally on your device.

Data collection

Holiday Game Night does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

The app does not require an account or sign-in and does not collect names, email addresses, location data, contacts, advertising identifiers, or usage analytics.

Player names and game progress used within the app are stored locally on the device and are not transmitted to the developer or any third party.

Third-party advertising and tracking

Holiday Game Night does not contain third-party advertising and does not use tracking technologies for advertising or profiling.

Children's privacy

Because the app does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. Any updated policy will be published at this location.

Contact

For privacy questions, please use the developer contact information provided on the Holiday Game Night App Store listing.
